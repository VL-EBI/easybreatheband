Privacy

Privacy and site analytics

This site counts visits so we know which songs and pages people enjoy. The data is anonymous, stays with Easy Breathe and is never used for ads. You can turn it off anytime.

What we count

  • the pages you visit, how far you scroll and how long a page is open
  • the links and buttons you click, and roughly where on the page
  • the songs you play and how much of each you hear
  • the website you came from, a campaign tag in the link (for example, from our hold music) and your screen size

We use this only to see what people enjoy and improve the site.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email address or IP address with these counts, and we don’t use cookies or follow you to other websites. Each visit gets a random ID that is forgotten when you close the tab. Nothing is sold or shared with advertising or analytics companies.

Kept deidentified

The analytics data is deidentified: it can’t reasonably be linked to you or your device. Easy Breathe commits to keep it that way and not to try to re-identify it, and requires any company that processes it for us to do the same.

Where it’s kept

The data is stored in Easy Breathe’s own account with Cloudflare, which processes it on our behalf. It is deleted after 13 months.

Your choices

Analytics are on unless you turn them off. Your choice is saved in this browser. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics stay off.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the Easy Breathe newsletter, your email address goes to Easy Breathe and its email service so we can send it. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Questions

For more about how Easy Breathe handles personal information, read the Easy Breathe privacy policy, or email privacy@easybreathe.com.
