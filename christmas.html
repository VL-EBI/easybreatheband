Home for the holidays

However you spend Christmas, we’re glad you’re here.

These songs are about the people who make room for us: the one who noticed first and stayed, the kids who can’t wait until morning, and the strangers who share a cookie tin when the flight is delayed. Wherever you are this year, we hope they keep you company.
