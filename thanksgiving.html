Thanks for spending a little of your holiday with us.

EBB started as a way to celebrate the people we talk with every day. These songs are about the parts of Thanksgiving that stay with you: packing an overnight bag, saving pie for breakfast and thanking the people who never ask for thanks.

Track 03

Keep the Good

3:08
