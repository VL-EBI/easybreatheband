About the project

The band is fictional. The reason behind it is real.

EBB's members, backstories and concert scenes are fictional. The music was created using AI in collaboration with Easy Breathe's CEO, inspired by the hopes, frustrations, humor and gratitude people share about life with CPAP.

This band is not real. Its members, backstories, and concert scenes are fictional. The music was created using AI in collaboration with Easy Breathe’s CEO. This is a creative project from Easy Breathe, the company that helps people get CPAP equipment, navigate prescriptions and insurance, and find support along the way.

But the feelings behind the music are real.

Since Easy Breathe was founded in 2011, we’ve listened to people share what living with sleep apnea and using CPAP means to them. Every day, we hear from people who tell us how much CPAP has changed their lives for the better. Those conversations—and the hopes, frustrations, humor, and gratitude within them—are the inspiration for this project.

We also know there can be hesitation. Wearing a mask. Sleeping beside someone. Wondering what other people will think. Something that helps you can still feel like something you need to hide.

We want to help change that.

We’re continually struck by how many people who may need help with sleep apnea haven’t been tested or started treatment. We hope this music starts conversations, makes people feel less alone, and encourages more people to explore testing and treatment with a healthcare professional.

And we hope it makes you smile. Maybe dance. Maybe hear a song about a CPAP mask and think, “That’s actually pretty good.”

Easy Breathe is a business, and we’re not hiding that connection. But our passion for making CPAP easier is genuine. It comes from the people we speak with and the difference they tell us it has made.

We made a fictional band to celebrate something real: getting help, being yourself, and having nothing to hide.

Please noteThe band’s stories are not real customer testimonials, medical advice, or promises of a particular result. Everyone’s experience is different. A qualified healthcare professional can help you decide whether sleep apnea testing or CPAP treatment is appropriate for you.
