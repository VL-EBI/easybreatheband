The band

Meet the band.

Cal, Nina, Ben and Tom share a love of good songs, a familiar rehearsal room and the conviction that there's always one more version worth trying. Together, they're EBB: warm, soulful songs and a sense of humor. Their music makes room for affection, uncertainty, everyday routines and the things people sometimes feel they need to hide. CPAP happens to be part of their world.

Swipe to meet everyone →

Lead vocals and acoustic guitar

Cal Benton

Brings the heart.

Cal leads with a warm voice and a stubborn sense of hope. He believes the next song could be the one that gets people listening, and he's happy to play it one more time to find out.

Bass and backing vocals

Nina Brooks

Keeps everyone grounded.

Nina brings a steady groove, a confident voice and a dry sense of humor. She keeps the band moving and usually has the best line in the room.

Guitar and keys

Ben Halton

Has another idea.

Ben hears possibilities in every song. A different chord, another harmony, a keyboard part nobody asked for yet. His enthusiasm tends to come with one more arrangement idea.

Drums

Tom Kellan

Is ready when they are.

Tom is easygoing, observant and quietly particular about his routines. He keeps the rhythm steady, knows where everything belongs and catches more than the others realize.

About the project

The band is fictional. The reason behind it is real.

EBB's members, backstories and concert scenes are fictional. The music was created using AI in collaboration with Easy Breathe's CEO, inspired by the hopes, frustrations, humor and gratitude people share about life with CPAP.
